# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith, critique work rather than people, and keep
discussions about the project.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualised language or imagery, publishing
someone's private information, and sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may ban participants who break these rules.
Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in SECURITY.md; reports are
handled confidentially.

For a fuller reference, see the Contributor Covenant: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/
